# Landing v2

## 2026-11-21

- The hills now fill the first screen on phones and desktop and stay behind every page as a fixed wallpaper, with a stepped parallax transition, a dim layer while reading, and a light snap on mobile.
- New `sina.exe` hero with name, role line and pixel-art CV / Contact / Blog shortcuts; the `▼ SCROLL` hint sits above the taskbar.
- Latest posts (up to five) leads the landing, with a narrower About me window showing status and a "now" list.
- The XP taskbar is on every page; it stays visible on the landing and slides away while reading elsewhere.
- Minimize sends a window to its taskbar button with a stepped outline; close removes it. Closing every content window offers the scenery view.
- Mobile title bars stick while scrolling through long windows.
- Reworked phone art: portrait hills, chunkier pixels, an evenly spread night sky, and better-placed clouds.
- Theme switches play a stepped sunset or sunrise; the sun now stays in the day sky, sharing the moon's spot.
- Contact is a card table (`klondike.exe`) with a waving pixel Sina, social shortcuts and copy on the felt.
- The blog is a mail program (`inbox.exe`): year folders, unread markers, a pinned preview on desktop, a phone-style mail list, and message windows with Copy link and Reply.
- Sub-page buttons gained pixel icons; mobile list text is smaller and landing excerpts stay on one line.
